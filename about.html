About me

Hello — I'm Joe. I write software for a living and draw for fun, and I've never quite managed to keep the two apart.

I'm a developer based in London. Most of my working life has been spent on the back end: services, data, and the unglamorous plumbing that keeps other people's software upright. I like problems that look boring from the outside and turn out to have a genuinely interesting shape once you're in them.

I was born in the 80s, which explains a fair amount about this website. My first machines beeped and loaded from tape, my first heroes came in floppy paper issues, and I still think a good sprite sheet is a work of art. The neon and the halftone dots up there aren't ironic — that's genuinely the decade my taste got stuck in.

What you'll find here

This isn't really a blog. I don't post on a schedule and I've no interest in pretending otherwise. Instead, when I build something I'm pleased with, I write it up in the project log — what I made, why, and what I'd do differently. Sometimes that's a side project, sometimes it's a technique I want to remember.

The other half of the site is the gallery. Comics and video games are my two long-running hobbies, and drawing is where they meet. Ink, digital, the occasional pixel piece — it all ends up in there.

Elsewhere

LinkedIn is the only social account I keep up with, so that's the best place to reach me. My code lives on GitHub.
